How it works

From customer message to automated resolution or supervised agent handoff.

1

Customer asks

Inquiries arrive through the website chat widget, browser voice call, or inbound support email.

2

Agent checks tools

Detects issue intent, verifies customer phone number, and retrieves verified order or tracking data from SQL.

3

Answer or ticket

Answers routine questions from indexed FAQs; routes billing disputes and sensitive claims to human teams with SLA tracking.

4

Weekly insights

Categorizes complaint volume and identifies recurring customer issues in automated weekly summary reports.
